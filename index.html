MODULE 01

Introduction to RPA

DEFINITION

RPA (Robotic Process Automation) ek technology hai jo software robots (bots) use karke repetitive, rule-based computer tasks ko automatically perform karti hai — bina human involvement ke.

Simple words mein: Jo kaam tum roz ek jaisa karte ho computer par — wahi bot kare tumhari jagah.

🧠 REAL-LIFE ANALOGY

Sochlo ek peon ki job: Roz subah 9 baje, woh aata hai, same files uthata hai, stamp lagata hai, forward karta hai. Har din same process. Ab imagine karo ek robot peon — jo kabhi thakta nahi, kabhi galti nahi karta, 24/7 kaam karta hai. Yahi hai RPA bot.

WHY COMPANIES USE RPA

⚡ Speed

Bot ek kaam seconds mein karta hai jisme human ko minutes lagein.

🎯 Accuracy

Zero manual errors — data entry, copy-paste sab 100% accurate.

💰 Cost Saving

Ek bot = multiple employees ka kaam. Operational cost drastically kam.

🔄 Scalability

Peak time mein 10 bots run karo, slow time mein 1. Flexible scale.

REAL-WORLD USE CASES

1

Customer loan application form aata hai email pe.

2

INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — MODULE 01
Q1. RPA kya hai? Define karo simple words mein.
RPA (Robotic Process Automation) ek software technology hai jo rule-based, repetitive digital tasks ko automate karne ke liye software robots use karti hai. Ye UI-level automation hai — matlab bot usi tarah screen use karta hai jaise ek human operator karta hai.
Q2. RPA aur traditional automation mein kya difference hai?
Traditional automation requires API integration and code-level changes in backend systems. RPA works at the UI/presentation layer — koi system change nahi chahiye. Legacy systems ke saath bhi kaam karta hai bina modification ke.
Q3. RPA ke 3 main benefits batao.
1) Speed — tasks milliseconds mein complete, 2) Accuracy — zero human error, 3) Cost Efficiency — operational cost 40-70% reduce. Plus: 24/7 availability, compliance/audit trail, scalability.
Q4. RPA ka use kab nahi karna chahiye?
Jab process unstructured ho (judgment required), jab data format frequently change ho, jab AI/cognitive tasks involved hoon (understanding context, natural language decisions). RPA best works for structured, rule-based, high-volume tasks.
◆ STRONG ANSWER — INTERVIEW READY
"RPA is a non-invasive automation technology that uses software bots to mimic human interactions with digital systems — performing repetitive, rule-based tasks at scale without modifying existing IT infrastructure."

Real-world use case: HDFC Bank ne RPA use kiya loan processing ke liye — processing time 2 days se ghatkar 4 hours ho gayi aur accuracy 99.9% ho gayi.
